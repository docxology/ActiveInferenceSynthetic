# Purpose and acceptance — Active Inference: A Synthetic Overview

## Purpose

Provide a compact, executable introduction to Active Inference and synthetic
intelligences in equilibrium and non-equilibrium thermodynamic and informational
settings. Use worked examples to connect inference, action, statistical
boundaries, and self-organization to the Free Energy Principle and Bayesian
mechanics. Give readers a clear route into `fep_formal` for deeper mathematics.

Synthetic owns a standalone paper, its teaching sequence, numerical models,
figures, tests, and metadata. `fep_formal` owns the generic mathematical reference
and reusable proofs. Synthetic intelligence is an application perspective;
current examples do not establish empirical intelligence or biological adequacy.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each demonstration has an explicit carrier, parameters, hypotheses, units,
  analytic reference, and a real boundary or negative control.
- Numerical cores and tests collect independently of the research template.
- Figure and manuscript values are generated from shared source parameters.
- Deterministic drift is distinguished from stationary probability current;
  diffusion quantities require positive dissipation.
- VFE, EFE, and thermodynamic free energy are interpreted within their own
  models; no equivalence is inferred from shared vocabulary.
- Formal exposition imports one immutable `fep_formal` main revision with
  matching Lean/Mathlib pins, complete axiom output, and no admissions.
- Upstream hypotheses and unproved transfers remain visible, including the
  Gaussian-blanket and planner correspondence boundaries.
- The real-computation suite meets the configured 90% branch-coverage floor;
  numerical, native proof, rendered-paper, and publication evidence are distinct.
- PDF/HTML outputs are regenerated and reviewed separately through the template.

extensions. Completion is established by actual receipts, not these criteria.
